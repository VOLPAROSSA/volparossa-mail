Image and asset credits

The Harbour of Letters scenes were generated on 17 November 2026 from the selected design reference, then exported into local responsive AVIF, WebP and JPEG assets. They are illustrative scenes, not evidence of a deployed network.

The Thunderbird-style inbox is a synthetic HTML illustration with fictional .test addresses. It is not a screenshot of a working VOLPAROSSA integration. Network connections and labels are HTML and SVG.

Upstream projects

Thunderbird is an independent upstream project. Its unmodified product mark links to its official website. VOLPAROSSA Mail is not officially associated with Thunderbird or Mozilla. Source record · Notice · Trademark policy.

Stalwart is a separate upstream mail server. Its name is used for accurate attribution; no Stalwart artwork is reproduced.

Icons, fonts and shared design

Interface icons are unmodified Lucide SVG geometry under the ISC licence. A few simple envelope, document and server diagrams are original SVG. The VOLPAROSSA mark, Inter and Cormorant Garamond fonts and shared navigation come from the pinned official website theme. Font and theme licences are included with the local assets.

Generation prompts, original masters, crop coordinates and SHA-256 provenance are retained in the source repository, outside the published artifact.
