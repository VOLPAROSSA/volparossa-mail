Planned integration

The intended design encrypts private messages before peers hold them, so a recipient can retrieve them when available.

  1. {{encrypt_picture}}

    Encrypt

    Encrypt the complete private message, including sensitive headers, before it leaves for custody peers.

  2. {{hold_picture}}

    Hold

    Peers hold encrypted message objects while the recipient is unavailable.

  3. {{retrieve_picture}}

    Retrieve

    The recipient retrieves the message and imports it into their own mailbox.

  1. Stored by peers
  2. Imported into mailbox
  3. Read by recipient

Stored does not mean imported. Imported does not mean read.

A missing acknowledgement must not silently trigger a second delivery through ordinary email.

  1. 1

    Submit only the reviewed public text to cooperative intelligence.

Encrypted peer custody

{{document_icon}}

Explicit AI sharing

Only reviewed public text is submitted through the current cooperative adapter. Private mail stays outside that public workflow.

A trusted mail host and a ciphertext-only storage peer have different roles.

What this means for ordinary email

Encrypting stored mailbox bodies does not hide ordinary SMTP reception, queued copies or metadata from the receiving operator. Private offline Internet reception needs a separately implemented and verified receiving boundary. It is not established by the current connector test.

What works today. What comes next.

One isolated Stalwart test establishes a specific connector result. It does not establish a complete mail service.
